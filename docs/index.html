ABOUT PROPERTY STUDIO

Explore and plan in 3D.

All dimensions describe this demonstration model. One scene unit equals one metre. This is a concept planner, not a survey or a permit drawing.

Garage design

Choose a fence, set a wall setback, and tap to draw the garage width and depth. Edit the garage and loft heights, add doors or windows, and position openings with their sliders. The driveway can connect a garage door to the rear alley and remove intersecting fence panels.

Measurements

Area uses the exterior footprint and excludes the loft. House clearance is a horizontal wall-to-wall measurement. Roof overhangs are excluded. The 10 ft spacing target is a planning preference, not a verified zoning requirement.

Touch controls

Drag to orbit. Use two fingers to pan and pinch to zoom. In plan view, drag to pan; two fingers work while drawing. Open Controls for dimensions and layers. At ground level, drag to look and hold an arrow to walk.

Keep your work

Completed edits save automatically in this browser and return when you reopen this site. Export a JSON file for a backup or to load your design on another device. No account, analytics or upload service is included.
